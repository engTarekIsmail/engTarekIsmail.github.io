Adventure Works Sales Dashboard (Excel)

An interactive Excel dashboard on product sales performance, built with a Star Schema data model in Power Query as part of an Excel Data Modeling course.

Excel Power Query Star Schema
1 / 1

Overview

The dashboard gives a view of sales across products, categories, subcategories, and cities, covering 2011 to 2014.

Visuals

  • Total sales by category, broken down by year.
  • Top 10 products by revenue.
  • Monthly sales trend against the running total.
  • Orders and sales share by category: Bikes lead with 39% of orders and 41% of sales.
  • Ship methods split.

Interactive Slicers

  • Order date timeline, product color, and product category.
  • City / territory.

Tools & Stack

Excel Power Query Star Schema modeling
