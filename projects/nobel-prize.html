Nobel Prize Dashboard

An Excel dashboard with a visual analysis of Nobel Prize awards: 1,000 prizes and 992 laureates, filterable by category, gender, and country.

Excel Dashboard design
1 / 1

Overview

Headline numbers: 1,000 prizes tracked, 992 laureates, Medicine as the top category with 227 awards, the University of California as the top organization, and the United States as the top country.

Charts

  • Prizes per category, top countries, and top 10 awarding institutions.
  • Average age at award by category.
  • Trend of annual awards over time.
  • Prizes by gender.

Key Insights

  • The United States leads with 291 laureates, more than the next 9 countries combined.
  • 91% of laureates are male and only 6% female.
  • Medicine has the most prizes (227), closely followed by Physics (225).
  • The University of California leads all institutions with 36 laureates.
  • Average age at award ranges from 57 (Physics) to 67 (Economics).
