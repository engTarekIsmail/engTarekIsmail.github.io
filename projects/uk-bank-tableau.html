UK Bank Customers Dashboard

A Tableau dashboard that analyzes the UK Bank Customers dataset across demographics, regions, job classifications, and account balances.

Tableau
1 / 1

Key Features

  • Regional analysis: customer distribution across England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland.
  • Demographics: gender split and age group segmentation using dynamic bins.
  • Job classification: White Collar vs Blue Collar vs Other.
  • Balance analysis: balance distribution across customer segments.
  • Interactive parameters: dynamic bin sizes for Age, Balance, and Customer ID.
