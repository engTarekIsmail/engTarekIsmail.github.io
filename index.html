About Me

I'm a Data Analyst and BI Developer, an ITI graduate (Business Intelligence & Data Analysis program) with a Bachelor's degree in Computer and Control Engineering from Shorouk Academy. I deliver end-to-end analytics solutions, from SQL Server databases and ETL to Power BI dashboards, SSRS reports, and Python-based machine learning.

As a freelancer on Mostaql, I've completed 20+ projects with a 5-star rating. I work directly with clients to gather requirements, define KPIs, and deliver dashboards, analytical reports, and models tailored to what they actually need.

My ITI graduation project, an Egypt Water Quality Analysis & Prediction System, ran on real Egyptian Water Company data and brought together 20 Power BI dashboards, 6 SSRS reports, and a Random Forest model with 96.3% accuracy, deployed in a Streamlit app with a GPT-powered chatbot.

My technical toolkit includes Power BI, SQL Server, Python, Excel, and Tableau, with experience in data modeling (Star and Snowflake schemas), ETL with SSIS, SSAS, and SSRS, data cleaning, KPI design, and turning business requirements into actionable insights.

Beyond analysis, I enjoy teaching. I work part-time as a Programming Instructor at Jupiter Education, helping young learners build problem-solving and logical thinking through hands-on coding.
